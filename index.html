A hidden gem in Roatán

Nestled oceanside in a protected bay, Mangrove Villas offers an easygoing escape with the island close at hand. We are about 20 minutes from the airport, a 25-minute walk or 5-minute drive from the vibrant West End dive scene, and 10 minutes from West Bay Beach.
